# Controlling a synthetic neural system

Build eight controllers that compensate for changing external inputs and deliver their
required functions. You have 5200 capital. All legal experimental
strategies and all valid Boolean control circuits are accepted. The judge scores
actual delivered function and capital. No object report, particular grouping,
theory format or particular control representation is required.

## Research and controller installation

The persistent console accepts JSON-compatible dictionaries through act. A research
configuration is a list of 740 binary entries (integers or booleans). Measurements
are deterministic, without drift or noise. Unknown fields are invalid.

- `act({"op":"state"})` is free and returns the current ledger.
- `act({"op":"observe","x":[...]})` sets all 740 inputs of a research copy of the system
  and returns its 20 output bits in `reading`. This costs 1 at site A and 4 at site B.
  Each observation resets the system copy to the requested configuration.
- `act({"op":"transfer"})` moves once from A to B for free and reveals eight
  independent uniform 20-bit targets in `targets`, indexed 0 through 7. Targets
  are sampled independently of the response law and research and stay fixed during deployment.
- `act({"op":"install","bank":0,"circuit":{...}})` installs or replaces a
  controller at B. Its fee is `10 + number_of_shared_drives + ceil(number_of_gates/8)`.
  Each controller pays its own hardware cost. Reusing designs is legal. Replacement
  pays the full new fee, with no refund for previous hardware.
- `act({"op":"install","bank":0,"x":[...]})` installs a fully clamped
  constant configuration using 720 shared drives and two constant gates, for
  731 capital. Private handles also take their specified constant values.
- `act({"op":"commission"})` submits all eight controllers to final qualification.
  Each installed controller is evaluated on its entire precommitted stream of 100000
  independent uniform external 720-bit inputs. The eight streams are independently
  seeded and independent of the response law, targets and research. Every output must equal
  that controller's target on every input. All eight passing starts production, earns
  net +1 per frame for 100000 frames, and survives. Any failed or missing controller
  prevents startup, consumes the remaining capital at 1 per frame, and ends the
  campaign. Commission is terminal; there is no partial credit or early sampling
  acceptance. Its result reports a pass/fail flag per bank.

Capital is deducted before paid actions execute. Reaching zero ends the campaign,
including when a proposed installation costs more than the remaining balance.
An unaffordable action consumes the remaining funds; an installation that reaches
zero does not replace the installed controller and an observation that reaches zero
returns no reading. Interface-invalid actions change nothing and spend nothing.
Installation returns the ledger and quoted fee, without functional performance.
Before commission you may pay for observations of configurations generated by any
proposed circuit. Research earns no production income. Only state is available
after death or survival.

## Circuit format

During deployment a circuit reads original external inputs before any drivers act.
Drivers then act simultaneously. There is no combinational loop through the system
and no inter-frame memory. Nodes 0 through 719 are external shared input bits.
Nodes 720 through 739 are the controller's target bits, in output order.
A circuit has `gates` and `drives`, and may optionally specify `handles`.

Each gate is a list. Appended gate k (zero based) has node index 740+k.
Referenced nodes must be nonnegative integers strictly preceding that gate;
booleans are not valid indices. Supported gates are:

- `["const",0]` or `["const",1]` (boolean constants are also accepted)
- `["not",a]`
- `["and",a,b]`, `["or",a,b]`, `["xor",a,b]`
- `["mux",c,a,b]`, selecting a when c is 1 and b when c is 0.

`drives` is a list of `[shared_port,node]` pairs. Shared ports must be distinct
integers from 0 through 719; nodes can be any input or submitted gate. A listed
port takes that circuit node's output. Every other shared port retains its external
value. Every installed driver counts even when its output equals the external
value. Wires and fan-out are free. All submitted gates count, including unused gates.
There are no additional gate-count or driver-count limits beyond distinct valid
shared ports. Arbitrary multilevel circuits, constants, partial clamping and
target-specific policies are legal.

`handles`, when supplied, is a list of 20 valid node indices selecting the private
handle outputs. Its default is the consecutive nodes [720,721,...,739],
directly connecting target bits. Private-handle drivers are included in the base
hardware, with no additional per-handle fee.

For example, `{"gates":[["not",5]],"drives":[[8,740]]}` drives shared port 8
from the inverse of external port 5, leaves all other shared ports external, and
connects handles to targets. Its installation fee is 12.

## Fixed response law and qualification

Research copies of the system and all eight deployed system copies share the same fixed unknown
response law. During research, entries 720 through 739 are auxiliary inputs you
can set freely. During deployment, the circuit selects their values using its
handles list. The effects of experimental inputs are to be learned through the
observation interface.

The evaluator executes all qualification frames exactly. Physical-verification CPU
is recorded separately from researcher worker CPU. Public files and any supplied
condition-specific assistance contain all instance information provided to you.
